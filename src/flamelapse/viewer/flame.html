    About this benchmark 

    
      
    Costs are counted CPU instructions from Valgrind's callgrind, not timings, so two runs of the same commit agree to within 0.02%. A change here is a real change in the code, not noise.
    
      
    Commits between the ones shown were measured too, so a jump marked "in this commit" is pinned to that exact commit.
